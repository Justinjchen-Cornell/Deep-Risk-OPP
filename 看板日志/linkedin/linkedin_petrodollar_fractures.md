🧭 1974: A secret deal between the US Treasury Secretary and Saudi Arabia. Oil in dollars. Dollars into Treasuries. Treasuries prop up the dollar. The Petrodollar was born. 📝

2026: Every leg of that cycle is breaking. Here are the FOUR structural fractures. 💥

---

📌 FRACTURE 1: The US is no longer an oil importer

1974: high oil = cost. 2026: the US is the #1 producer. High oil = revenue.

BUT the flip side: US borrows $155B EVERY MONTH. Debt service: $24B EVERY WEEK. Annual interest now exceeds $1T - more than the defense budget. Deficit heading toward 7% of GDP.

The world's largest credit card. 💳

---

📌 FRACTURE 2: Japan - the most loyal buyer - is now a SELLER

1996-low levels: JGB 10Y at 2.87%. 30Y approaching 4%. Debt/GDP at 218%, heading to 239%.

The 30-year global carry trade - borrow in Japan at zero, buy US assets - is UNWINDING. Japan's central bank just spent $50B defending the yen.

As carry unwinds, global funds sell US Treasuries to repay yen loans. That alone pushes US yields up 15-40bp.

The most reliable buyer of US debt for 50 years is now the most dangerous seller. 📩

---

📌 FRACTURE 3: Saudi Arabia - the founding partner - is pricing oil in YUAN

Since Jan 1, 100% of Saudi crude to China is settled in RMB. ~40-50% of Saudi-Latin America oil trade in RMB. Middle East-China energy trade: 40% RMB now.

RMB is now the #2 settlement currency in Middle East oil - the first time in 50 years. The euro got squeezed out.

Saudi joined mBridge (central bank digital currency cross-border settlement). China's banks tripled lending to Gulf states - making them the largest lender there. You lend in RMB, they price in RMB. 💱

The first leg of the petrodollar loop - oil sold for dollars - is LOOSE. 🎷
