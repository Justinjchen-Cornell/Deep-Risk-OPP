**This is the best buying opportunity of the cycle.**

---

## Phase 4: The Flood (Christmas 2026+)

The Fed reopens the liquidity spigot. Dollars flow back out. Commodities enter their main rally. Gold goes on its annual bull run. Oil enters a second long-term upswing.
