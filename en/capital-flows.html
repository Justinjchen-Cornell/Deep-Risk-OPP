Why This Matters

The 2026 thesis: the US needs to refinance $40T of debt. To do that, it must attract global capital into Treasuries. The mechanism: open the Middle East inflation valve → global capital flees weak currencies → floods into USD → Treasury issuance succeeds → offshore dollar freeze (centripetal collapse) → distressed assets get absorbed → dollar re-deployed.

The three flows are the seismograph for every stage of this cycle.
