First / Second / Third-Order Opportunities

OrderDefinitionExample
FIRST Everyone sees the event. Everyone makes the same trade. Profit gone. Oil up → everyone buys energy stocks
SECOND The transmission chain others have not priced yet. Oil up → chemical costs rise → pair trade energy vs airlines
THIRD Structural consequences most people are not thinking about. Oil up → Fed hikes → CRE defaults → short regional banks

First-order is crowded — skip it. Second-order is the execution zone. Third-order is where cognitive advantage converts (use <2% positions).
